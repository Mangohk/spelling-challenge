# Letter Hero — Design (teaching-games / Primary 1)

## Goal

Calm letter recognition (A–Z / a–z) for Primary 1 EFL / SpLD students, shipped in **Mangohk/teaching-games** under Primary 1 — not Spelling Hero.

## Format (match other teaching-games)

- Folder: `letter_hero/`
- Files: `index.html` + `index.jpg`
- Hub card under Primary 1 with `data-skill="alphabet"`
- Tailwind CDN + Fredoka / Quicksand
- Chunk `btn-3d` buttons, sky “clouds” background
- Back link: `../index.html`
- Title pattern: `Letter Hero - P1 EFL Game`
- Bump hub stamp in UTC+8 when shipping

## Modes

Find It · Match Friends · Hear & Tap · Surprise Mix  
Case: ABC / abc / Aa Bb · 10 rounds · soft retry · slow TTS

## Out of scope

- Spelling Hero combat / equipment
- Merging into `spelling-challenge`
